**Companions:** [Creator economy](BLACKOUT_CREATOR_ECONOMY.md) · [Coalition game](BLACKOUT_COALITION_GAME_PLAN.md) · [App plan](BLACKOUT_APP_PLAN.md) · [BMC financial analysis](https://github.com/Blackmarket-coa/free-black-market/blob/8b7f0b63f1d0e4856f5f5ea5afd17549e51ad60c/docs/BMC_ECOSYSTEM_FINANCIAL_ANALYSIS.md)

## Current revenue catalogue

| Revenue family | Examples | BMC accounting |
|---|---|---|
| Original creator products | Downloads, guides, stream assets, music/sounds, original companion art, cosmetics, validated quests/learning trails and community templates | Single FBM 3% fee on third-party sales. Pack price belongs to creator. |
| First-party enhancements | Original cosmetics, publishing/broadcast convenience, supported organizer/creator tools, quest authoring and workspace enhancements | One-time product proceeds after refunds; costs separate. Existing enhancements are founder-owned according to the current FBM launch plan. |
| Finite creator access/work | Individual content unlock, ticket, workshop, commission, bounty or defined community/cohort product | Actual eligible FBM transaction terms; creator receipts remain separate. |
| Finite sponsorship | Disclosed shows/events, learning activities, original-world content and contextual public discovery | Explicit BMC delivery fee only where BMC has that contract; participant/reward budgets are restricted funds. |
| Storage/resources | Additional storage/archive capacity with defined terms | Blackout recurring storage remains a proposal, not an approved platform subscription model. No Blackout storage profit is forecast here. |
| Finite services/support | Setup, training, event production, branding, custom integrations and licensed distribution work | Delivered BMC services only; community-delivered work is third-party income. |
| Voluntary funding, affiliations and hardware | Eligible donations/grants, disclosed referrals, original physical goods | Excluded from scenarios until actual terms, demand, costs and fulfillment exist. |

Native software enhancements and supported content packs use one-time purchases. Recurring creator memberships remain a separate unresolved policy choice and are excluded from this model. No AI personas, paid voting power, paid contribution rank, fee discounts for status, or paid ranking in chronological Circle/Reach feeds.

Blackout and FBM offer overlapping gameplay connected through canonical places, communities, activities and supported content. Gameplay can create commerce demand without making every encounter a purchase. Free discoveries and earned native products produce no cash sale.

## Conditional ecosystem projection

| Scenario | BMC revenue, Jan–Dec 2027 | Operating surplus before founder pay/tax |
|---|---:|---:|
| Downside | $1,461 | -$2,492 |
| Base | $17,318 | $7,630 |
| Upside | $125,802 | $84,334 |

These are consolidated ecosystem numbers, not additional Blackout revenue. The base allocates approximately **$1,877** of first-party product revenue to Blackout using 35% of a shared product-unit pool at a proposed $15 average price. Download/game commissions are already inside the ecosystem's $5,098 retained marketplace fee. Do not add Blackout's 3% to FBM's 3%.

The 26,000 social followers are acquisition opportunities. Actual signups, activation, repeat purchasing and local availability are unmeasured. Base paid creator downloads start in assumed month 4 and paid creator game packs in month 7 of 2027; these are financial gates, not release commitments. No unverified revenue should be treated as booked or committed.

## Priority order and measurement

1. Verify one first-party original enhancement: payment, working unlock, restore and refund.
2. Finish production creator payload/upload and signed-delivery wiring, persistent authoring drafts and content/offer associations.
3. Verify one Coalition encounter/collection, then author/install an original pack before selling it.
4. Confirm seller settlement and sponsor-funded obligations with actual money movement.
5. Calibrate the forecast with delivered products, unique paying customers, refunds, provider costs, creator earnings and repeat purchases.

Before native digital checkout, verify region/program requirements, exact allowed billing route, restoration and cost allocation. A 15% or 30% distribution fee must be explicitly modeled; a fixed 3% marketplace fee cannot quietly finance an extra native charge.

Historical subscription tiers, 95/5 splits, governance access charges and configuration-only go-live claims in older revenue documents are superseded for current planning. Their source descriptions do not establish current runtime readiness. The app/creator/game plans and this financial analysis govern the new direction; live migrations require separate implementation and verification.

## Monthly targets and direct game economics

The [monthly target and launch analysis](https://github.com/Blackmarket-coa/free-black-market/blob/8b7f0b63f1d0e4856f5f5ea5afd17549e51ad60c/docs/BMC_REVENUE_TARGETS_AND_LAUNCH_PLAN.md) separates revenue, direct contribution and operating surplus. Under its base candidate prices/refunds/channel costs, 75 BMC-owned $15 Blackout product sales provide about $1,008 of contribution before common operating costs, founder compensation and tax. Each is a new eligible purchase; one-time rights are not renewed monthly.

Third-party creator game packs generate approximately $74 in annual BMC commission in the conditional base forecast, before separately incurred game development and maintenance. BMC-owned packs belong in first-party revenue, not the creator-fee total. Any game-driven acquisition, retention or commerce improvement requires measurement before it can increase the forecast.

Keep one complete encounter and supported creator-delivery path ahead of broad raids/AR expansion. Free participation, earned products and contribution recognition remain independent of purchases. The new target calculations do not change the existing annual scenarios or approve prices.
