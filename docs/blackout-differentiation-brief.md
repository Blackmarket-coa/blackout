# Blackout Differentiation Brief (Go-to-Market Proof Artifact)

## Positioning statement

Blackout is the cooperative governance + secure communication layer for organizations that need both privacy credibility and accountable collective decision-making.

## Differentiation pillars tied to shipped capabilities

## 1) Stego tiers (privacy depth by risk posture)
- Tiered steganographic carriers (emoji/image) with payload caps, corruption handling, and rollback controls.
- Operational value: discreet coordination pathways without requiring server-side decode trust.

## 2) Governance reputation + broadcasts
- Signed governance payload attestation with deterministic rejection reasons.
- Governance audit summaries for operators and transparent accountability.
- Operational value: decisions are verifiable, not just discussable.

## 3) Federation as coordination multiplier
- Cross-community governance broadcast flows with failure-handling runbooks.
- Coalition onboarding from independent org to federated governance network.
- Operational value: local autonomy with network-level coordination.

## 4) Platform bridges roadmap
- Maintain category focus today (governance + secure comms).
- Expand interoperable bridges deliberately (not all-in-one suite sprawl).
- Operational value: extensibility without product identity drift.

## Buyer translation

| Buyer concern | Blackout proof |
